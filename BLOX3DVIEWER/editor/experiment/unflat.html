Isometric voxel extractor — upload & click calibration

Steps:
  1. Upload the image.
  2. Click the top center of a voxel you choose → this becomes Origin (0,0,0).
  3. Click a neighboring voxel top in the +X direction (one step over).
  4. Click a neighboring voxel top in the +Y direction (one step over in the other grid axis).
  5. Click a voxel top that is one layer higher (same i,j but +1 k) → defines +Z step.
  6. Set max height & thresholds, then click Analyze.

Calibration points (in order): Origin, +X, +Y, +Z.

How to Use Controls

This is an augmented reality viewer for 3D models. The app uses your device's camera to display the models in your environment. Here's how to interact with them:


Model Selection:

  • Tap on a 3D model to select it and activate the transform controls.
  • Tap on an empty area of the screen to unselect the model.

Transform Controls:

  • Use the Move button to drag the selected model to a new position.
  • Use the Rotate button to rotate the selected model around its axes.
  • Use the Scale button to resize the selected model.

Camera Controls:

  • One finger: Orbit around the scene.
  • Two fingers: Pan (move the camera).
  • Pinch gesture: Zoom in and out.

Note: The camera controls are disabled while a model is selected for transformation.
